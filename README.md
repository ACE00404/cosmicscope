# 🌌 CosmicScope

**Explore how the night sky would look from worlds beyond our Solar System.**

CosmicScope is an interactive astronomy visualization platform that allows users to explore exoplanets, compare their characteristics, and visualize constellations and star patterns from different perspectives across the galaxy.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

When we look up at the night sky from Earth, the constellations we see are shaped by our unique position in space.

But what if you were standing on a planet orbiting another star?

Would Orion still look the same? Would familiar constellations even exist?

CosmicScope aims to answer these questions by providing interactive visualizations of exoplanets and stellar positions, allowing users to explore how the universe might appear from different locations beyond Earth.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🌍 Exoplanet Explorer
Browse and explore a collection of exoplanets along with their key characteristics.

### 📊 Exoplanet Comparison
Compare multiple exoplanets side-by-side to better understand their differences in size, distance, and other properties.

### 🌠 Interactive Star Maps
Visualize constellations and stellar arrangements from different perspectives.

### 📈 Data Visualizations
Explore exoplanet data through interactive scatter plots and graphical representations.

### 🌌 3D Exoplanet Views
View planetary objects in an immersive 3D environment.
